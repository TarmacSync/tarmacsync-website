Airport Procurement Resources

A curated reference of official sources relevant to U.S. airport procurement. These links point to FAA, government, and industry resources — not TarmacSync content. For regulatory questions, always confirm with your ADO, counsel, or the applicable official source.

FAA Guidance

FAA Airport Improvement Program (AIP)

Official AIP homepage with program overview, eligibility, and guidance.

AIP Handbook (Order 5100.38D)

The FAA's primary guidance document for AIP grant administration, including procurement and contract provisions.

FAA Advisory Circulars

Advisory Circulars on airport design, construction, equipment, and operations — including procurement-relevant guidance.

Grant Assurances

The obligations airport sponsors accept when receiving AIP grants. Currently 40 Grant Assurances covering procurement, operations, civil rights, and reporting.

Federal Procurement Rules

2 CFR Part 200 (Uniform Guidance)

Federal procurement standards applicable to AIP and other federal grant-funded purchases. See § 200.317–200.327 for procurement methods.

Cooperative Purchasing

GSA Cooperative Purchasing

Information on using GSA Schedules through cooperative purchasing, including IT Schedule 70 eligibility for state and local governments.

Industry Organizations

American Association of Airport Executives (AAAE)

Professional organization for airport executives. Offers accreditation (A.A.E.), training, and industry resources.

Airports Council International – North America (ACI-NA)

Trade association representing commercial service airports in the U.S. and Canada.
