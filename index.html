[ 05 // Early access ] Commercial service · Reliever · Public-use GA

Built for the teams responsible for moving airport projects.

Who is TarmacSync for?

TarmacSync is designed for U.S. airport operators and sponsors, including commercial-service, reliever, and public-use general aviation airports. Typical users include airport management, procurement, finance, operations, engineering, grants, and capital-program staff.

Is TarmacSync replacing our procurement system or ERP?

No. TarmacSync is the intelligence layer that sits above the systems your airport already uses. It helps teams evaluate the path before a requisition, purchase order, solicitation, or contract workflow begins.

Your existing ERP, procurement platform, contract repository, and grant-management tools remain in place.

What kinds of purchasing decisions can it help with?

TarmacSync helps teams compare routes such as informal quotes, formal bids, cooperative contracts, existing agreements, negotiated professional services, emergency procurement, and other policy-authorized methods — across 50 airport procurement categories from ARFF vehicles and snow removal equipment to terminal construction and concessions.

Does TarmacSync make the final procurement decision?

No — deliberately. It organizes the facts, surfaces candidate paths, explains tradeoffs, and identifies what still needs validation. Final decisions remain with authorized airport staff and any required legal, governing-body, or grant-agency reviewers. TarmacSync never uses words like "compliant" or "approved."

How does it use FAA and federal requirements?

TarmacSync connects project and purchase decisions with relevant FAA guidance, Grant Assurances, 2 CFR Part 200 considerations, Buy American requirements, DBE obligations, and other applicable program rules — with citations retrieved from governed sources, never generated from thin air.

It does not replace formal FAA concurrence or legal review.

Can it account for our airport's local procurement policy?

Yes — this is core to the product. Import your procurement policy and TarmacSync evaluates each purchase against your own thresholds, delegated authority, board requirements, solicitation rules, emergency provisions, and other local procedures.

Can TarmacSync help identify cooperative contracts?

Yes. TarmacSync indexes 800+ cooperative and government purchasing vehicles across the major consortiums, each indexed with federal guidance. It helps the team evaluate scope, pricing, authority, funding compatibility, and documentation — always framed as candidate pathways to validate before award.

What information would our airport need to provide?

Early access typically begins with selected non-sensitive materials such as procurement policies, purchasing thresholds, representative templates, relevant contract information, and one or two real-world workflows. Setup takes minutes, not weeks.

Is airport information used to train public AI models?

No. TarmacSync is designed for controlled enterprise use, not as a public-data training pipeline. Specific data handling, retention, and model-provider terms are documented before any airport is asked to provide production information.

Why not just use a general AI assistant?

A general AI assistant can summarize a document or answer a one-time question — and will confidently invent a regulation when it doesn't know one. Pathfinder is an agentic system built for this domain: it actively searches your airport's own policies, the federal knowledge base, 800+ cooperative contracts, and live FAA sources before answering; every regulatory claim cites its retrieved source; and the airport's persistent working context — policies, thresholds, projects, funding signals, contract candidates, evidence — carries through the entire purchase.

What happens after I join the waitlist?

We confirm your role, airport type, and the workflows you want to improve — then follow up personally. Selected teams are invited to early product conversations or design-partner evaluations, where founding airports directly shape the roadmap.

Is there a cost to join?

No. Joining the waitlist is free and creates no commitment. Any future paid early-access scope and pricing would be discussed transparently before participation.
