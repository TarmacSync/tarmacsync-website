Who is TarmacSync for?

U.S. airport operators and sponsors — including commercial-service, reliever, and public-use general aviation airports. The primary users are airport management, procurement, finance, operations, engineering, and grants staff.

Is this replacing our procurement system?

No. TarmacSync is the intelligence layer before and around those systems. It connects purchase planning, funding signals, airport policy, grant readiness, and reporting follow-through while required staff, legal, grant, and board reviews remain in place.

Why not use a general AI tool?

General AI can summarize a document or answer a one-time question. TarmacSync is designed around the airport’s ongoing working context — its profile, policies, capital projects, contract candidates, funding signals, source evidence, open questions, and reporting obligations — so the team can return to the same project and keep moving it forward.

What happens after I join?

We will confirm your role, airport type, and the purchase workflows you want to improve. Joining the waitlist does not promise immediate product access; selected teams will be contacted when the early-access workflow is ready for evaluation.

Is there a cost to join the waitlist?

No. Joining the waitlist is free and does not create a commitment. If a paid early-access program is offered later, scope and pricing will be discussed clearly before you decide whether to participate.
