Questions you might have.

Who is the 90-day pilot for?

U.S. public-use airports or sponsors — especially GA, reliever, non-hub, and small-hub airports. Teams with a real upcoming procurement or project who want to help shape an airport-specific tool while getting direct founder support.

What would our airport need to provide?

Non-sensitive materials only: your procurement policy, purchasing thresholds, and background on one or two representative procurements or projects. No SSI, CUI, classified, or protected security information.

Does TarmacSync replace our existing systems?

No. TarmacSync is the intelligence layer that helps your team evaluate the path before a requisition or solicitation begins. Your ERP, eProcurement, contract, and grant-management tools remain in place.

How does TarmacSync handle airport information and source material?

TarmacSync connects purchase decisions with relevant FAA guidance, Grant Assurances, and other official sources — with citations retrieved from documents, not generated. Airport information is not used to train public AI models. Approved service providers may process information to operate the service under disclosed data-handling terms. TarmacSync does not replace FAA, legal, or board review.
