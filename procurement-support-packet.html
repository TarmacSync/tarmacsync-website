What TarmacSync does

TarmacSync helps U.S. airport teams turn a purchase or project need into a clear working procurement path, the evidence to review, and the next action — before the process gets stuck.

Pathfinder starts with a plain-English purchase or project need, connects the relevant airport and funding context, and helps the team understand a working procurement path, material open issues, supporting evidence, and the next action. It guides, organizes, researches, and drafts; airport staff and applicable authorities continue to review and decide.

What the team gets

  • A clearer working path based on the purchase, anticipated value, funding, airport policy, and available contract context.
  • Material open issues surfaced before they become delays.
  • Grounded evidence from reviewed airport documents and current official sources when it affects the answer.
  • A practical next action that keeps the project moving.
  • A connected project file that can support planning, procurement, grant, and closeout work over time.

What TarmacSync is not

  • Not an ERP, bid portal, accounting platform, e-signature service, or official records system.
  • Does not select vendors or obligate funds.
  • Does not issue final procurement, grant, legal, or FAA determinations.
  • Does not replace legal or procurement review.

How to evaluate TarmacSync for your airport

  1. Review the public trust, security, and privacy pages at app.tarmacsync.com/trust. Confirm the security posture and data-handling commitments meet your airport's requirements.
  2. Review the Data Processing Addendum for subprocessor and cross-border transfer details. US-only processing is available on request.
  3. Confirm the pilot data boundaries. The Founding Airport Partner Program uses non-sensitive project and procurement information. SSI, CUI, classified, and export-controlled material are not accepted.
  4. Identify a real purchase or project expected during the next six months. The strongest evaluation is working through an actual airport need.
  5. Schedule a fit conversation. Complete the form at tarmacsync.com/#application. Omar will reply personally within two business days.
For your procurement or purchasing team: TarmacSync is priced per airport, not per user, starting at $2,400 per year. A W-9 is available on request for vendor setup. Government Customer Addendum and Data Processing Addendum are provided for public-agency review. Terms page: tarmacsync.com/terms.html.
