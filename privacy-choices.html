Privacy Choices

TarmacSync does not sell personal information or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Your privacy rights

You may contact TarmacSync to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with you. Certain information may need to be retained for security, contractual, public-records, backup, dispute-resolution, or legal purposes.

How to submit a request

To submit a request, use the TarmacSync contact form. Do not include passwords, payment information, government identification numbers, or sensitive airport information in the initial request.

Verification and non-retaliation

TarmacSync may need to verify a requester's identity before completing a request. Requesters will not receive discriminatory treatment for exercising an applicable privacy right.

More information

For full details on how TarmacSync collects, uses, and shares information, see our Privacy Policy.
