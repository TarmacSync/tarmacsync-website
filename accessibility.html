Accessibility Statement

TarmacSync is committed to making airport procurement intelligence usable by people of all abilities. This statement describes our accessibility standard, the current state of our evaluation, and how to request help.

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Our standard

TarmacSync targets WCAG 2.2 Level AA across its public website and authenticated workspace. Accessibility is considered in product design, development, testing, and documentation.

Current status

Accessibility validation is ongoing. TarmacSync has not yet completed an independent accessibility audit or claimed full WCAG conformance.

Current evaluation methods include automated accessibility testing, keyboard testing, responsive-layout review, semantic HTML review, and accessibility-focused code review.

Accessibility features

Across the public website, TarmacSync currently provides:

  • A visible skip-to-content link on every page.
  • Semantic landmarks and a single primary heading per page.
  • Descriptive link text and associated form labels.
  • Visible keyboard focus indicators.
  • Keyboard-operable video controls with accessible names.
  • Open captions embedded in the vision film.
  • Reduced-motion support.

Known work in progress

Current accessibility work includes:

  • Assistive-technology testing with VoiceOver, NVDA, Safari, and Chrome.
  • Keyboard review of authenticated product workflows.
  • Focus behavior in dialogs and layered interfaces.
  • Zoom, reflow, text-spacing, and high-contrast testing.
  • Accessibility of generated Word documents and PDF exports.
  • Independent review of the Accessibility Conformance Report.

Video accessibility

The TarmacSync vision film contains open captions embedded in the video. If you need the film's content in another format, contact us using the method below.

Non-discrimination

TarmacSync is committed to equal access and non-discrimination. Read our Non-Discrimination statement.

Assistance and feedback

If you experience difficulty using TarmacSync or need information in another format, please email contact@tarmacsync.com. Include the page or feature involved and, if comfortable, the browser or assistive technology you were using.

We aim to acknowledge accessibility reports within two business days.
