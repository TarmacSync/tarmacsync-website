Airport Procurement Intelligence

Airport procurement involves multiple systems, rules, and stakeholders. Funding sources each carry their own compliance requirements. Contract options exist across cooperative consortiums, state schedules, and existing agreements. Policy thresholds vary by airport. FAA guidance must be considered alongside local authority.

TarmacSync brings these pieces into one guided workspace — so your team can evaluate the likely path before the process starts, see what still needs validation, and keep the entire project record connected as work advances.

What TarmacSync connects

  • Procurement policy. Your airport's thresholds, delegated authority, board requirements, and competition rules.
  • Funding sources. AIP, BIL, PFC, state, and local funding — with the compliance stack each triggers.
  • FAA requirements. Grant Assurances, 2 CFR Part 200, Buy American, DBE obligations, and Advisory Circulars.
  • Contract options. Cooperative vehicles, state schedules, and existing agreements screened against your purchase and funding context.
  • Project record. Decisions, evidence, open issues, and next actions in one connected file.

Designed for U.S. airports

TarmacSync is built specifically for the workflows, terminology, and regulatory environment U.S. airport operators navigate daily. It is not a generic procurement tool adapted for aviation — it was designed from the start around airport purchasing, funding, and compliance.
