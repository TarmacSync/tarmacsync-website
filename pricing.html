How to evaluate the value

Compare the work, not just the subscription.

A useful evaluation compares the effort your team spends today with the effort required using TarmacSync. Include setup, research, verification, corrections, and preparing the next handoff.

Setup effort

Record the time required to configure airport policy, thresholds, roles, and permitted source material.

Research and preparation

Compare time spent gathering the purchase facts, funding context, policy, contract options, and supporting evidence.

Verification and corrections

Include reviewer time, source checking, corrections, unresolved questions, and the effort required when facts change.

Handoff quality

Check whether the next reviewer receives a clearer working record, open questions, and a usable next action.

Staff capacity released is not the same as cash savings. Any financial comparison should use your airport’s actual loaded labor cost, volume, setup effort, and review burden. No customer savings result is claimed here.

Common questions

Do you charge per user?
No. Pricing is per airport.
How is TarmacSync priced?
Public plans are annual agreements priced per airport, with no per-seat pricing. Invoicing and payment timing can be documented in the order form, subject to your airport’s purchasing requirements and agreed terms. The subscription term remains annual.
Which tier is my airport?
Start with your airport profile: Field for general aviation and reliever airports, Regional for nonhub primary airports, and Metro for small hubs. Medium and large hubs or major capital programs are scoped individually.

These are starting points for discussing scope. Project complexity, funding, and support needs may change the fit. FAA hub categories depend on a share of national enplanements, not fixed passenger ranges. See the FAA airport category definitions.
What budget typically pays for TarmacSync?
Airport operating funds may be one possible budget source. Your airport determines the appropriate budget classification, purchasing authority, and payment path.
How does a product evaluation work?
A product evaluation is a separate, time-boxed process around a real, permitted, non-sensitive airport project. Scope, users, success measures, data boundaries, and any fees are agreed in writing before access begins.
Can I pay by credit card?
Payment method is confirmed during contracting. Do not assume card payment is available; your airport’s purchasing policy and the agreed order terms govern.
Can we pay by purchase order or check?
Purchase-order or check payment may be arranged during contracting, subject to vendor setup, your airport’s purchasing policy, and agreed terms.
Do you provide a W-9?
A W-9 can be provided during vendor setup, subject to confirmation.
What happens to our data if we cancel?
Your airport can export all projects, decisions, and evidence files at any time, during or after a subscription. An authorized workspace owner can request a full organization export.
