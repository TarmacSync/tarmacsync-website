How airports buy TarmacSync

A software subscription is normally paid from airport operating funds, not from a federal grant, which means your own adopted purchasing policy governs the purchase. We set our tiers with those thresholds in mind so most airports can acquire TarmacSync through the simplest method available to them.

TierTypical path
Field Purchasing card or direct purchase order. Monthly billing is available where a lower per-transaction limit applies.
Regional Direct purchase order as a small purchase. Published airport authority policies commonly set a small-purchase ceiling at or above $15,000.
Metro Direct purchase order as a small purchase, with headroom below common ceilings. Where a local limit sits lower, quotes or a single-source justification apply.

Your adopted purchasing policy governs and may be more restrictive than the examples above. TarmacSync does not make procurement determinations for your airport — we supply documentation to support your price-reasonableness file, a single-source narrative where your policy permits one, or a response to a formal solicitation, whichever your process requires.
