Common questions

Do you charge per user?
No. Pricing is per airport, and your outside consultants can work in the file at no additional cost.
Is annual cheaper than monthly?
Yes — paying annually is the equivalent of two months free. Monthly billing keeps each transaction well inside typical purchasing-card limits.
Which tier is my airport?
Tiers follow your FAA airport category, which is set by annual enplanements. Under roughly 10,000 passengers — general aviation, reliever, and nonprimary commercial service — is Field. Roughly 10,000 to 500,000, the nonhub primary range, is Regional. Roughly 500,000 to 2.5 million, small hub, is Metro. Above that, medium and large hubs are scoped individually. FAA sets the hub cutoffs as a share of national traffic, so the exact figures shift a little each year.

Capital activity can outrun category — a general aviation field running a large federally funded project has more to manage than its size suggests. If that is you, tell us what is coming up and we will tell you which tier fits.
Is a subscription an allowable grant cost?
That depends on the grant and how the cost is treated. Confirm with your grant administrator and FAA ADO before assuming it — most airports purchase from operating funds.
What happens at the end of the 90 days?
You decide whether to continue. Nothing converts automatically, and there is no renewal to cancel.
Do you offer multi-airport or consultant pricing?
Yes. Multi-airport pricing is available for authorities, county and municipal systems, and state aviation offices. Consultants and program managers can license TarmacSync across client engagements.
