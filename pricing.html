Common questions

Do you charge per user?
No. Pricing is per airport.
Is annual cheaper than monthly?
Yes — paying annually is the equivalent of two months free. Monthly billing is available for budgeting convenience. Your airport's policy, delegated authority and total contract commitment determine the appropriate purchasing path and whether a P-card may be used.
Which tier is my airport?
Tiers follow annual enplanements. Under roughly 10,000 — Field. Roughly 10,000 to 500,000 — Regional. Roughly 500,000 to 2.5 million — Metro. Above that, medium and large hubs are scoped individually.

A GA airport with a large federally funded project has more to manage than its category suggests. If that is you, tell us what is coming up.
What budget typically pays for TarmacSync?
Most airports purchase TarmacSync from airport operating funds. Your airport determines the appropriate budget and purchasing path.
What happens at the end of the 90 days?
You decide whether to continue. Nothing converts automatically, and there is no renewal to cancel. Founding airports that keep TarmacSync lock in their launch price for the first 12 months.
Can I pay by credit card?
Yes. Credit card payment is accepted through Stripe billing. A payment link can be sent to you directly.
Can we pay by purchase order or check?
Yes. Purchase orders and checks are accepted. A payment link via Stripe is also available if your airport prefers to pay by credit card.
Do you provide a W-9?
Yes. A W-9 is available on request for your airport's vendor setup.
What happens to our data if we cancel?
Your airport can export all projects, decisions, and evidence files at any time — during or after a subscription. An authorized workspace owner can request a full organization export.
